## Attribution discipline

USER-CONFIRMED means the recovered registry explicitly labels the origin that way.
LIKELY-USER means the recovered registry used that weaker attribution; it is not silently upgraded.
MIXED means the recovered registry does not attribute the final wording solely to one participant.
ASSISTANT-LABEL means the recovered registry explicitly labels the wording as assistant-labeled; do not present it as user-coined.
UNRESOLVED means provenance/currentness is not sufficiently recovered.
