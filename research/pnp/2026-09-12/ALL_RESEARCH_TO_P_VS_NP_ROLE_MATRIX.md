## Current empirical consequence

The whole-ecology finite experiment found that random and deliberately periodic/Shor-like generators did not produce size-6-avoiding partial tables on the tested four-variable NAND surface. Exact Partial-Hard selection, affine-rank/geometry selection, a diverse mutation, and local restriction/NOR carriers did.

The Pareto ablation showed two currently consequential generator families:
- removing `local_nor` loses the maximum finite gate-hardness and the shortest gate-7+ certificate;
- removing `geometry_rank_minority` loses the low-locality/nonlocal gate-7 frontier;
- other generators did not uniquely change the declared frontier metrics in this finite panel.

This does not make either family an asymptotic P-vs-NP route. The local-NOR family is already known here to scale only linearly in the completed calibration, while the geometry family currently reaches only bounded gate 7.

## Admission rule

`proposal -> counterprobe -> exact verifier -> barrier check -> uniform theorem -> only then resolution claim`
