# Hodge Decision-Field Evidence Firewall

Status: `ACTIVE_BOUNDARY`

This Hodge branch may reuse general research-control patterns such as one-degree probes, typed residual states, decision partitions, counterprobes, provenance, and planner/verifier separation.

It may **not** import any P-versus-NP theorem, SAT experiment, complexity heuristic, lower-bound claim, range-avoidance result, or solver performance measurement as evidence for algebraicity or for the Hodge conjecture.

Likewise, Hodge results do not become evidence for P versus NP.

Allowed relation:

```text
shared method schema
!=
shared mathematical evidence
```

Cross-project references must be marked `METHOD_ONLY`, `ANALOGY_ONLY`, or `NO_EVIDENCE_TRANSFER`.

Hodge claims require Hodge-native definitions, sources, computations, constructions, and proofs.
