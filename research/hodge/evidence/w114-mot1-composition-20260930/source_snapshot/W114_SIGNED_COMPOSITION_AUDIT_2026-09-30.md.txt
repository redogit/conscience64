## Closed projective primitive with the quotient included

Work in homological `Chow(K,Lambda)`, K=Lambda=Q(zeta_114), with the subgroup
mu_57 and its character embedding H_a=G_(2a). A graph acts by pushforward and
its transpose by pullback. Cycle composition is rightmost first.

For (d,n)=(114,2) or (57,3), put k_i=i*d/n, i=1,...,n-1, and

`Y_a = tensor_i h(F_d^(2))^(a,k_i)`,
`X_a = h(F_d^(n)<n>)^(a,...,a)`.

Use the actual varieties supporting these projectors. On each Fermat-curve affine
chart, `Q:(u,v)->(x=u,y=v^(d/n))` maps to `C:x^d+y^n=1, x!=0`.
Let `U` be the product of these open Fermat charts, and use the published maps

`C^(n-1)->T: s_i=prod_j(1-zeta_n^i*y_j), t=prod_j x_j`,
`X_open->T: s_i=t_i^d, t=prod_i t_i`.

Define `Z_a=U x_T X_open`; **barZ_a is the closure of this finite
fiber-product correspondence in the projective Y-support x X-support**.
This specifies a cycle, rather than taking a graph of an abstract motivic map.
Define e_Y, e_X by the stated characters; e_sym is the identity for n=2
and `(1+swap)/2` for n=3.

| n | deg Q | deg C^(n-1)->T | deg X->T | F:Y->X | G:X->Y |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---|---|
| 2 | 57 | 1 | 114 | e_X [barZ] e_Y | (1/6498) e_Y [barZ]^t e_X |
| 3 | 361 | 114 | 3249 | (1/3249) e_X [barZ] e_sym e_Y | (1/20577) e_Y e_sym [barZ]^t e_X |

These include Q_* in F and Q^*/deg(Q) in G, relative to the earlier transfers on
C^(n-1). Thus GF has exact scalar

`c_F*c_G*deg(Q)*deg(C->T)*deg(X->T)/(n-1)! = 1`.

The reverse composition is identity on the target using the cited invertibility
and the same nonzero maps; this is theorem use, not a proof from scalar arithmetic
alone. Omitting Q's inverse factor gives scalar **57** or **361**. Omitting the
inverse symmetrizer factor gives **1/2**. These failed proposals are retained.

For target boundary t0=0 the diagonal exponent is n*a, and for each ti=0 the
coordinate exponent is a. For the source curve boundaries the exponents are
a and a+k_i. All are nonzero in the eight declared cases. The quotient characters
are trivial on its kernel since n*k_i=0 mod d. Coordinate intersections inherit
a projector-zero stabilizer. Projective closure uses the same projectors and
the source's localization theorem; no arbitrary unprojected boundary correction
is assumed to vanish.

## Signed D2 composition and its actual endpoints

Retain the historical ordered occurrences

`+D2_7, +D2_22, -D2_23, -D2_56, +N_1, -N_2`.

For a positive D2 use G_a:X_a->Y_a; for a negative D2 use F_a:Y_a->X_a.
The four **valid** D2 edges, lifted with identities on every spectator, give

`B = B_4 o B_3 o B_2 o B_1`,

`P_f tensor X_7 tensor X_22 tensor Y_23 tensor Y_56`

`-> P_f tensor Y_7 tensor Y_22 tensor X_23 tensor X_56`.

Its inverse is the reverse sequence of inverse edges. These are products of a
fourfold and four curves, so B is a dimension-8 cycle in the product of the two
dimension-8 supports, acting in motivic weight 8. Every endpoint and spectator is
checked; an arbitrary terminal relabeling as W114 is rejected.

After removing the coefficient-2 twists, the **source/target** Gauss-symbol ratio
is the four signed D2 terms, with exponent +100. The actual twisted endpoints
contribute exponent -100. Thus B's actual realizations agree, as an isomorphism
requires. Treating +100 as B's unexplained realized residual would reverse this
normalization.

The source plane z_f=e_f[L], L:x0+x3=x1+x5=x2+x4=0, retains pairing 1/57
with z_bar_f and P_f=57(z_f x z_bar_f). Therefore P_f and the product block are
nonzero under the recorded plane intersection formula and transfer theorems.
Precisely, B acts as `z_f tensor v -> z_f tensor B_curves(v)`.
**Bare z_f is not an input to B. No W114 plane push is claimed.**

The N1-N2 occurrences remain in the exact arithmetic ledger and are rejected as
characteristic-zero geometric leaves. They have not been silently canceled out
of the requested full correspondence. The four n=3 closures are also recorded
