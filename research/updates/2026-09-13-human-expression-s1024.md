## Two carrier branches, explicitly separate

`SP1024-1` is the existing archive's raw-byte transport. A full 1,024-byte section uses 133 finite float64 carriers: 1,064 binary payload bytes before framing. It is not compression of arbitrary data.

`S1024V1` is a separate boundary-conditioned UTF-8 rank codec. Its retained report gives 117 float64 carriers / 936 payload bytes for a complete section, and 1,030 bytes with standalone framing. That branch is preserved as a separate candidate; its experimental results were not rerun in this project-integration pass. It does not replace the archive's transport. No wire compatibility or combined savings is implied.
