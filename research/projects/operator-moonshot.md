# Project — Operator Moonshot

Status: `ACTIVE_RESEARCH_LAB_WITH_EXPLICIT_CLAIM_CEILINGS`

## I — What do we have?

A long-running research laboratory for bounded mathematical, computational, educational, and operator experiments. It includes structured solver work, Computer Mind / Operator-Mind experiments, semantic-kernel work, education experiments, and later open-problem calibration.

## R — What difference matters?

The central distinction is:

```text
interesting mechanism or positive calibration
!=
replicated result
!=
general scientific claim
!=
solution of an open problem
```

Calibration families must remain distinct from open targets. A successful finite or synthetic experiment can guide the next test without inheriting authority it did not earn.

## P — What should we do next?

1. Require source/evidence separation for every claim.
2. Replicate before promotion.
3. Preserve failed replications as first-class evidence.
4. Use calibration families only for calibration.
5. Keep open-problem claims below the strongest independently supported boundary.
6. Apply one-degree perturbation and counter-probes before increasing complexity.

## O — What happened?

### Highlights

- The project evolved toward stronger evidence, provenance, ablation, replication, and claim-ceiling discipline.
- Operator-Mind / TaskWorld work produced bounded causal-search/reuse evidence rather than relying only on qualitative behavior.
- Computer Mind Gate 1B and related machinery became recoverable as explicit experiment states instead of a single narrative summary.
- Open-problem work increasingly separated known calibration results from genuinely unresolved targets.

### Lowlights / preserved corrections

- Cycle 3 failed independent replication and therefore was not promoted.
- Operator Moonshot Education E3 failed robustness, requiring a narrower E3.1 repair/retest path rather than treating earlier success as sufficient.
- Earlier research sometimes tried to connect too many mechanisms before establishing the smallest causal relation.
- Some original source material remains a known recovery gap, including the original structured solver/operator-lab source.

## Checks

**What are we assuming?** That the experiment's target, baseline, and evidence carrier actually measure the claimed relation.

**How do we test it?** Replication, ablation, adversarial counterexamples, independent verification, and explicit comparison against simpler baselines.

**What do we still not know?** Which mechanisms generalize outside their finite/synthetic domains and which are artifacts of the chosen task, representation, or evaluator.

## Claim ceiling

Operator Moonshot is a research process and evidence ledger, not evidence that its larger theoretical targets have been solved.
