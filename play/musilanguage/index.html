Lyrics / performance cues
What do the floats contain?
MUSIC64/v1 uses the 52 fraction bits of an ordinary binary64 number in [1, 2). The fields describe six scale steps, rhythm, tonic, scale, tempo, meter, one of four compact ensemble families, treatment and intensity. The new 64-style profile layer lives in the recipe and changes arrangement density, swing, syncopation, register and orchestration while preserving old float addresses.
The listener’s mistake layer may flip only the lowest 26 bits: notes and rhythm. It cannot touch the float’s exponent, sign, style profile or source text.
Source text, exact UTF-8 coordinates, integer sampling rank and musical interpretation remain separate. At most 32 evenly spaced six-byte blocks become cells. Full accepted source is retained in the recipe; the music is lossy. This is an artistic instrument, not evidence of a universal language–music law. Read the specification and provenance.
The listener decides what is worth hearing. Source, predecessor pages, tests, and replay contracts remain preserved in Git history.
