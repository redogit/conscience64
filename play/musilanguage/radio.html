How the station works / limitations & free use

The music is synthesized locally from original note scores. There are no sampled recordings, paid music calls, or artist imitations. The original single is unchanged. Concept and musical direction: Ryan April. New composition, lyrics, and code: AI-assisted work in this conversation. You may use, perform, modify and share this release; see RADIO.md.

Conscience64 supplies public metadata through its documented postMessage API. Metadata is treated as data, never code. The music client checks both the sender’s origin and window, uses an exact target origin and bounded requests, and falls back to local music on failure. The selection-to-music mapping is an artistic design, not a validated sonification or evidence of historical Musilanguage.

Continuous playback runs in this page after your Play gesture. Closing the page, sleeping the device, or browser restrictions can stop it. Nothing is scheduled on a server. Pause, Stop and one-track mode remain under your control. This player retains at most two rendered tracks in its cache, plus temporary render/export memory.
