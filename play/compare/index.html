Source Compare

See exactly which lines changed between an original text and a revision.

Free to use · no account
How to use

Paste two versions, then compare their lines. Spaces, punctuation, and Unicode differences count. Up to 300 lines and 20,000 characters per version. Line-ending differences are reported separately. This does not judge meaning or translation quality.

Your work stays in this tab until you choose to save or export. Nothing you type is sent to a server.

Opening UTF-8 files preserves their line endings. Editing in the text boxes may normalize line endings.
