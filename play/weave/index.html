Word Weave

Arrange lines of writing. Keep the original beside every new version.

Free to use · no account
How to use

Write one thought per line, then move or shuffle the lines. Blank lines are kept. Up to 100 lines. Your original stays unchanged when you rearrange the remix.

Your work stays in this tab until you choose to save or export. Nothing you type is sent to a server.
