# Computational Chorus — “Keep the Remainder”

Original Conscience64 research song. The lyric is a mnemonic compression of project methods and boundaries. It is **not** evidence that the mathematical or cross-domain expressions are equivalent, solved, or proved.

## Verse 1 — Language

Meaning moves, memory maps, models meet the measure.  
Syntax shapes signals; semantics survives the switch.  
Subject stays, structure shifts, semantics still survives.  
Carrier crosses context; consequence continues; remainder remains.

## Pre-chorus — Method

Input. Relation. Plan. Output.  
Observe the change and keep the remainder.  
Life, learning, living belong inside the calculation.  
Cost counts, time turns, ethics edges every plan.

## Chorus

**Period. Enter. Tweet. Explode.**  
Compress the carrier; keep the code.  
Compare the change; counterprobe the claim.  
Carry what survives—don’t rename resemblance proof.

## Verse 2 — Complexity

Witness written, verifier works; search can still swell.  
NP hardness needs a reduction; resemblance is not reduction.  
P versus NP stays open; proof is pending, pattern is not proof.  
A shortcut on a selected space does not settle the whole.

## Verse 3 — Carriers

Unicode units, UTF-eight bytes, Float64 brings the bytes back.  
Bit identity is transport identity; truth travels by another track.  
Minimal repair: smallest shift, strict test, surviving structure.  
S-prime is a question until definitions, execution, and evidence answer.

## Bridge — Open mathematics

Hodge classes call for cycles; cycle spans call for proof.  
Deficits can direct the search, but coincidence cannot close the conjecture.  
Generate, verify, counterprobe, revise.  
A beautiful pattern is a beginning, not a verdict.

## Final chorus

**Period. Enter. Tweet. Explode.**  
Say it clearly; test the code.  
Compress. Compare. Counterprobe. Carry.  
**Claim only what survives.**

## Exact boundaries behind the lyric

- `Language Quality = meaning preserved / cognitive effort` is a working design measure.
- `O = F(I,R,P)` is task-local planning shorthand, not a fixed universal function.
- `x ∈ L ⇔ ∃w, |w| ≤ poly(|x|) and V(x,w)=1 in polynomial time` is the witness/verifier characterization used for NP.
- `P ?= NP` remains open.
- `source —transform→ target + remainder` is a carrier contract, not proof transfer.
- `[S′](TgI)=0 under minimal repair g` remains project notation/hypothesis until explicitly defined and verified.
- `verified subject / obligation ≠ implementation carrier` preserves the Tiny Babel subject/carrier distinction.
- Exact UTF-8 ↔ Float64 byte reconstruction establishes transport identity only.
- The Hodge conjecture remains open in general.
