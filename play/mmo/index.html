Reality anchors

The game art follows the structure of real observations without copying their scientific status.

  • EHT images show a bright ring around a central black-hole shadow.
  • The Milky Way center is crowded with stars, gas and dust—not empty black space.
  • Webb observes infrared light; published colors map invisible wavelengths into visible colors.
  • Dust lanes can hide stars in visible light while infrared observations reveal more structure.
