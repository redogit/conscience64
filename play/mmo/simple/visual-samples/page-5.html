Red Wilds · role transitions

Class-to-class shapeshifting

The fictional fiend can move between known jobs and classes as a gameplay/state transition: tools, obligations, evidence rules, and abilities change with the role.

Transition invariant: the public character remains fictional and non-identifying through every class change.

Transition grammar

FORM + ROLE + WORLD + TOOLSET + OBLIGATION → NEXT FORM

Examples: Researcher → Builder → Tester → Verifier; Data Specialist → Mapper → Accessibility Tester; Explorer → World Builder → Renderer → Storyteller.

Role/state morphing only; no private-photo morphing.
