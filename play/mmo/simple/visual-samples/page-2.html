Fiend of Shapeshifting

A non-identifying fictional many-form entity. Its visual language comes from freely reusable masks and environments, never from a private person's face.

Identity boundary: FICTIONAL_CHARACTER_ONLY · NO_REAL_PERSON_REFERENCE · NO_LIKENESS_RECONSTRUCTION.

Morph rule

Each form may change silhouette, material, mood, tools, class, job, and environment. No form may converge on or reconstruct a private person's identifiable appearance.
