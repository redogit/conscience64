The Many-Form Fiend

A privacy-safe shapeshifter for the expansive world: one fictional entity capable of assuming the known classes, jobs, tools, obligations, and play styles in the project.

Hard boundary: PRIVATE PERSON != CHARACTER. No private image, face, likeness, or identity may be published, reconstructed, inferred, or used as a public character asset.

World rule

The Fiend may shapeshift across regions, classes, jobs, tools, and carriers. Every transformation preserves provenance and the non-identity boundary. New forms should be built from fictional design or explicitly reusable public references only.

Current replacement sources are CC0/public-domain Wikimedia Commons material; source pages remain linked.
