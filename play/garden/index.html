Pattern Garden

Play with shapes and symmetry. Make a pattern you can take with you.

Free to use · no account
How to use

Select a cell to cycle through four states. Use arrow keys to move between cells, and Enter or Space to change one. Columns run left to right. Rotate, mirror, or try a new pattern. Undo reverses a pattern change.

Your work stays in this tab until you choose to save or export. Nothing you type is sent to a server.
