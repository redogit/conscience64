Small Steps

Turn what matters into one next step. Keep a record of what you tried and learned.

Free to use · no account
How to use

Name a goal, describe what you have, and choose a next step. After trying it, record what happened. A checkpoint keeps a snapshot in this tab; save or export to keep it after leaving. Up to 100 checkpoints. You decide what success means.

Your work stays in this tab until you choose to save or export. Nothing you type is sent to a server.
