Space Lens remains local-first and evidence-bounded. Master governs coordination and Compass4D governs navigation; neither is an evidence authority. The animated field is a timed 4D→2D visualization of interaction, retrieval, selection, and success aggregation; it is not a physical four-dimensional measurement.
